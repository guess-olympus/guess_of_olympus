GUESS OF OLYMPUS - PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use GUESS OF OLYMPUS or contact us. It covers local game data, support communications, and the Meta/Facebook SDK used for app analytics and advertising attribution.

1. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The game saves your chosen player name and avatar, settings, scores, puzzle collection, in-game coins, item inventory, check-in history and recent coin transactions on your device. This information supports gameplay and lets you return to your progress. You do not need an online account to play. Opponents and leaderboard sample entries are local simulations, not live profiles of other players.

The core game does not upload your local save file as a cloud-save service. Local gameplay storage is separate from the analytics and attribution information described below. Your device or operating system may include app data in backups according to your own backup settings.

2. META/FACEBOOK ANALYTICS AND ADVERTISING ATTRIBUTION

The app uses the Meta/Facebook SDK to understand app usage, measure app events and evaluate the effectiveness of advertising campaigns that promote the game. This can involve transmitting information to Meta when a network connection is available.

Depending on the events enabled in the app, SDK configuration, applicable law and your choices, this information may include app launches and activity events, event times, app and SDK versions, device model, operating system, language, IP address, device or installation identifiers, crash and diagnostic data, and technical information related to event delivery. Where permitted, it may also include an advertising identifier and information used to attribute an installation or app event to an advertising campaign.

On iOS and iPadOS, we request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing the advertising identifier or engaging in tracking that requires that permission. If you allow tracking, information may be linked with information from other companies' apps or websites for advertising measurement. If you decline, we do not access the advertising identifier or perform tracking that requires your permission. Declining tracking does not prevent you from playing the game. Limited analytics or privacy-preserving measurement that does not constitute tracking may still occur where permitted by law and your choices.

Meta receives SDK data and processes it under its applicable terms and privacy policy. Its processing may take place outside your country. Learn more about Meta's practices and privacy controls at:
https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

The use of advertising attribution does not mean the game requires a Facebook login or offers online multiplayer.

3. INFORMATION YOU SEND TO SUPPORT

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your message, device details or screenshots. We use this information to answer questions, investigate issues and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information or other sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your request.

Email providers may process messages to deliver and store our support correspondence. We do not use support messages for advertising targeting.

4. WHY WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to operate the game, retain local progress, understand usage, improve reliability, measure advertising effectiveness, provide support, protect our services and comply with applicable legal obligations. Where consent is required for analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking, we rely on that consent. Other processing may be necessary to provide requested services, pursue legitimate interests where legally permitted, or comply with law.

5. SHARING AND INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

Information may be processed by Meta for the purposes described above and by service providers supporting email and technical operations. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, security or safety. Service providers and Meta may process information in countries with different privacy laws; applicable transfer safeguards are required where the law calls for them.

Advertising-related sharing can be treated as a sale or sharing of personal information under some privacy laws even when no money is exchanged. You may contact us to exercise applicable rights concerning such processing and use the tracking controls described below.

6. STORAGE, RETENTION AND DELETION

Local progress remains on your device until you reset it or remove the app and its data. Use Settings > Reset local progress to reset game data. Removing the app does not necessarily remove device backups or information already sent to a third-party service.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, resolve follow-up issues and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of information we hold about you. Meta's retention and deletion practices are described in its own policy; resetting local game progress does not delete previously transmitted Meta data.

7. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

You can manage tracking permission in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Withdrawing permission affects future tracking and does not automatically delete information previously processed. You can also use Meta's privacy controls and contact us with a request relating to information we handle.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. You may also have the right to complain to a privacy regulator. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your request, but please do not send sensitive identity documents unless specifically needed through an appropriate process.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the age at which consent is required by applicable law, without the required authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app or support, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. SECURITY

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Please keep your device secure and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information in your chosen player name or support messages.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy when app features, services or legal requirements change. The updated date will appear above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before a material change takes effect.

11. CONTACT US

For questions about this policy, privacy choices, data requests or game support, contact us and mention GUESS OF OLYMPUS in your message.
